Welcome, Operator!

Complete lifting missions by collecting cargo from the pickup zone and carefully placing it in the delivery zone. Avoid dropping the load outside the target.

  • Use the sliders or keyboard/mobile controls.
  • Lower the hook to the cargo before picking it up.
  • Release the cargo inside the green delivery zone.

How to play

1. Rotate and move the trolley until the hook is above the blue cargo.
2. Use Hoist / cable to lower the hook. When the hook is close enough, press Pick / Release.
3. Carry the cargo to the green delivery zone. Adjust rotation, trolley and cable length.
4. Press Pick / Release to place it. Successful delivery unlocks the next mission.

Keep the load controlled. If you lose control, use Emergency stop or Restart.
